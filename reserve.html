Meet The Reserve Rules & FAQ

Woodpeckers Sneakers & Streetwear is committed to genuine culture and fair pricing. Here is everything you need to know about participating in the campaign.

Visit In-Store

Each participating item has a hidden minimum reserve threshold based on rarity, condition, and market deadstock value. When your offer meets or exceeds this threshold, the deal is automatically unlocked or rapidly approved by our team.

If your offer is close to the reserve floor, our team will send you a counteroffer. You can accept the counteroffer with one click or politely decline. If an offer is far below realistic market value, it will be declined with an opportunity to resubmit.

Accepted offers are reserved exclusively for you for 48 hours. During this window, your private checkout link lets you complete payment via MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, Card, or reserve for showroom pickup.

100% guaranteed. Every sneaker and streetwear piece in our collection goes through rigorous physical verification for materials, stitching, labels, packaging, and provenance before being listed.
