# 1. PROJECT GENESIS — HOW WE GOT HERE

I am Philip, a software developer and recent Computer Science graduate from KNUST. I am exploring opportunities to build professional websites for Ghanaian businesses and pitch those websites directly to the business owners.

I initially identified four potential businesses:

1. KIKI Clothing — https://www.instagram.com/kikiclothing/
2. Hyybrid — https://www.instagram.com/hyybridofficial_/
3. Button Up Thrift Shop — https://www.instagram.com/button_up_thrift_shop_gh/
4. Woodpeckers — https://www.instagram.com/woodpeckers_gh/

After an initial review of their Instagram profiles, Woodpeckers became the preferred prospect.

The reason was not simply that it sells shoes. Its social media activity revealed an interesting business opportunity.

Woodpeckers was promoting an unusual campaign called **Meet the Reserve**, which allows customers to select certain products and make offers rather than simply purchasing at a fixed displayed price.

This suggested an opportunity to create a website that could support its existing commercial activities and introduce a more organized digital shopping experience.

The decision was therefore to focus on Woodpeckers rather than continue developing generic websites for all four prospects.

IMPORTANT: This is initially a speculative website concept to be pitched to Woodpeckers. We do not yet have confirmation that Woodpeckers has commissioned the project, approved the design, supplied its assets, or agreed to any functionality.

We must build a convincing, brand-tailored concept without pretending to represent the business officially.
