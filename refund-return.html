Welcome to Woodpeckers we aim to ensure complete customer satisfaction. If you are not fully satisfied with your purchase, please review our return and refund policy below.

1. Return Eligibility

  • You may request a return if:
  • The product is unused, unworn, and in original condition
  • The product is returned with original packaging, tags, and invoice
  • The return request is made within [7/10/15] days of delivery

2. Non-Returnable Items

  • We do not accept returns for:
  • Used or damaged products (not due to our error)
  • Products without original packaging
  • Items marked as Final Sale / Non-returnable

3. Damaged Or Incorrect Products

  • If you receive:
  • A damaged product
  • A wrong item

Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos/videos. We will arrange a replacement or refund.

4. Return Process

  • Email us at contact@woodpeckersgh.com with order details
  • Our team will approve and provide return instructions
  • Our team will approve and provide return instructions

5. Refund Policy

  • Refunds are processed after inspection of returned item
  • Refund will be issued to the original payment method
  • Processing time: 5-10 business days

6. Return Shipping

  • Customers may need to bear return shipping charges (unless product is defective or wrong)

7. Exchange Policy

  • Exchanges are allowed based on product availability
  • Only for unused items within return window
