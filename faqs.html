Meet The Reserve & Weekly Drops

Everything you need to know about naming your price on exclusive grails at Woodpeckers Ghana.

Meet The Reserve is Woodpeckers Ghana's signature shopping initiative. Instead of fixed prices, selected grail sneakers and streetwear carry an undisclosed minimum acceptable price (the "Reserve"). You submit an offer representing what you are prepared to pay. The first offer that meets or exceeds the undisclosed reserve secures the item immediately.

New Reserve merchandise drops every Thursday, Friday, and Saturday. You can participate online right here on our digital portal or walk into our showroom at 24 Blohum Road, near Fidelity Bank, Dzorwulu, Accra to inspect pairs and submit offers in person.

When you submit an offer, you receive a unique Offer Reference code (e.g. WPK-RES-xxxx). If your offer meets the reserve, you will receive an instant acceptance notice and SMS/WhatsApp confirmation. You have 24 hours to complete payment at your agreed price and collect in Dzorwulu or request express delivery.
